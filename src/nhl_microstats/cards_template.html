Rush: the puck was recorded outside the attacking zone in the previous 8 s with no faceoff since. The NHL logs no zone entries, so play-by-play only catches about 6% of attempts as rush (video tracking finds 20–30%). Rush percentiles rest on small counts. Cycle: 10+ s of in-zone play since the last exit or faceoff, not a rebound or rush.

Not available from NHL data: passes, shot assists, zone entries and exits, entry denials, retrievals.

Goal tracking: from the NHL's puck and player tracking of the ~14 s before each goal, the data behind nhl.com's goal replays. It only exists for goals, so these count what a player did in the build-up to his team's goals: time with the puck, carries over the offensive blue line, defenders beaten (an opponent within 10 ft who was ahead of the carrier and ends up behind him), dekes (the puck swinging 2.5+ ft across his body within 0.5 s with an opponent or the goalie within 10 ft; not checked against video). Possession is inferred from the puck staying within 5 ft of a skater and moving with him. Ranked against 2025-26 only.
